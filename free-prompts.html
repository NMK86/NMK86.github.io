Chart Generation — Bar Chart with Outlier Highlighting

Turn raw sales data into a presentation-ready chart in under 15 seconds. No formulas, no drag and drop.

COPY & PASTE INTO JULIUS AI
TASK: Analyze sales performance by category
DATA: Connect your CSV or database — columns needed: Category, Monthly_Sales
OUTPUT: Bar chart sorted high to low
FLAG: Highlight categories more than 20% above average
1. DATASET — Upload your CSV or use sample data
2. CALCULATION — Compute average · flag outliers above threshold
3. VISUALIZATION — Sorted bars · color-coded · threshold line · clear title
4. INSIGHT — Top 3 categories · one recommendation · max 3 bullet points
How to use: Upload your CSV to Julius AI → paste this prompt → export the chart as PNG for your report.

Cohort Retention Analysis — Heatmap

Visualize customer retention across cohorts instantly. Identify which cohorts are underperforming before your next stakeholder meeting.

COPY & PASTE INTO JULIUS AI
TASK: Analyze customer cohort retention for a SaaS product
DATA: Connect your CSV — columns needed: Cohort_Month, Month_0 through Month_5
OUTPUT: Cohort retention heatmap with color-coded performance
FLAG: Highlight cohorts where Month_3 retention drops below 40%
1. DATASET — Monthly cohorts · Month_0 = 100% · realistic retention decline
2. CALCULATION — Retention % per cohort per month · identify best and worst cohort
3. VISUALIZATION — Heatmap · dark green (high) to dark red (low) · % values in cells
4. INSIGHT — Best cohort at Month_3 · overall trend · one recommendation
How to use: Export your cohort data as CSV → upload to Julius AI → paste prompt → get a presentation-ready heatmap in seconds.

KPI Dashboard Planning — What to Measure

Stop building dashboards with vanity metrics. Use this prompt to identify the 6-8 metrics that actually drive decisions for your stakeholders.

COPY & PASTE INTO CLAUDE
TASK: Plan a KPI dashboard for [stakeholder type, e.g. "weekly executive review"]
DATA: My available data sources are: [list your sources]
OUTPUT: Prioritized list of metrics with grouping and display recommendations
FLAG: Identify and remove any vanity metrics that don't drive decisions
1. METRICS — Identify 6-8 most important metrics for this audience
2. GROUPING — Suggest how to group them (revenue / operational / leading indicators)
3. FREQUENCY — Which metrics need daily vs weekly vs monthly view
4. VANITY CHECK — Flag metrics that look good but don't drive action
How to use: Run this in Claude first → then build your dashboard in Databox with the metrics Claude recommends.

Automated Report Template — Stop Sending Manually

If you send the same report every week, you're wasting time. Use this prompt to build a template you can automate — set it up once and it runs forever.

COPY & PASTE INTO CLAUDE
TASK: Write a weekly report email template I can automate
DATA: I send [report type] to [X] stakeholders every [Monday/week/month]
OUTPUT: Complete email template with subject line, metrics section, insight, recommendation
FLAG: Use plain language — no jargon — assume 90 seconds to read
1. SUBJECT LINE — Max 8 words · focused on the most important number
2. METRICS — 3-5 key numbers with week-over-week change
3. INSIGHT — One sentence on what changed and why it matters
4. NEXT STEP — One clear action for the reader
How to use: Run in Claude to get your template → set it up in GetResponse → it sends automatically every Monday. You never touch it again.
