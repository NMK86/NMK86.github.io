What Julius AI Cannot Do

It's worth being clear about the limits. Julius AI is a chart and analysis tool — it's not a full BI platform:

  • No live dashboards — charts are one-off exports, not real-time. For automated dashboards, use Databox.
  • No written narrative — Julius describes what the data shows, but won't write your executive summary. Use Claude for that.
  • Free tier limits — the free tier has a message cap. For daily use, the paid plan (~$20/month) is worth it.
